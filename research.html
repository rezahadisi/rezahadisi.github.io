Research

I work on questions in ethics, epistemology, action theory, and the philosophy of imagination, often by engaging historical perspectives. My primary interests in the history of philosophy are the medieval Arabic and Persian tradition, as well as Kant. These days, I am working on a large project on the philosophy of imagination and its relationship to action theory and moral epistemology.
